About Us

Welcome to VoCAFE, where high-energy beats, vibrant digital culture, and exceptional brew collide!

Born from a shared love for electronic music, colorful synths, and the legendary virtual singers who defined a generation, VoCAFE was built as a cozy, high-tech haven for fans, creators, and coffee lovers alike. Whether you're here to power through your next creative project, hang out with friends, or simply fuel up on caffeine that hits like a 190 BPM chorus, our doors are open to everyone.

From our signature Miku Miku Tea to Gumi Lattes, our menu serves up handcrafted coffee, vibrant drinks, and colorful pastries inspired by iconic virtual idols.

Drinks & Music

The Chimera’s Red Wing Fizz

Don't be fooled by its sleek appearance, this drink packs a punch! Featuring a vibrant, ruby-red blend of tart pomegranate, sweet dark cherry syrup, and a splash of fizzy lemon-lime soda, it perfectly captures Kasane Teto’s signature crimson look and high-energy personality. Served ice-cold over large rock ice, it’s crowned with a cocktail pick holding sugar-dusted blueberries, a nod to her quirky chimera wings and penchant for mischievous fun. Pairs best with a side of freshly toasted French baguette!

The World Is Mine Blue Milk Tea

Dressed in Hatsune Miku’s signature electric teal, this enchanting drink blends blue spirulina and butterfly pea flower with rich, creamy milk tea. Poured over a generous layer of chewy brown sugar boba pearls, it's piled high with fluffy whipped cream, vibrant teal sprinkles, and delicate dried edible flowers. It’s smooth, dreamy, and instantly recognizable, just like Miku’s unforgettable voice. Don't forget to take a picture before mixing, it’s camera-ready from every angle!


Music Chosen - World Is Mine

The Tsundere Citrus Blast

Match Akita Neru’s iconic side-ponytail and golden-yellow aesthetic with this vibrant, sunshine-hued refresher! Bursting with tangy citrus, it features a blend of freshly squeezed orange juice, sparkling lemon-lime soda, and a touch of passionfruit syrup served over crisp ice rocks. Garnished with a bright orange wheel and a glowing maraschino cherry, a sneaky nod to her favorite flip phone, it delivers a sharp initial kick that sweetens up as you sip. Perfect for sipping while typing out snarky comments or ignoring your notifications!

The Echoes Matcha Strawberry Latte

Channeling Gumi’s vivid green aesthetic and signature orange and red accents, this stunning tri-layer creation is a total crowd-pleaser. The base features a rich, sweet strawberry purée layered under silky whole milk, topped off with a smooth, vibrant pour of ceremonial-grade iced matcha. As earthy tea notes blend seamlessly with fruity sweetness, every sip delivers the same versatile, crossover energy that made Megpoid Gumi a legend. tir well before drinking to watch the layers hit a full-spectrum color shift, or enjoy it unmixed for a new flavor hit with every sip!
